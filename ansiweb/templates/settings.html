Steps that hang

{{ csrf() }}

An installer waiting on a dialogue nobody will answer, or a script that never returns, would otherwise hold the deployment until the whole job times out. After this, AnsiWEB stops waiting for that step: the part it belongs to is recorded as failed and the rest of the deployment carries on. Windows updates are not covered by it, since they are slow by nature.
