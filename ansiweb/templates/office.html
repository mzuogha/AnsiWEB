How Office is cached

Office can only be downloaded with Microsoft's Office Deployment Tool, which runs on Windows. AnsiWEB uses one managed PC with internet access (the helper PC) to download each new Office build, then copies it to this server. All other PCs install Office from this server, and receive Office security updates from it too (Office's own updater is pointed at {{ plan.update_url or 'http:///software/office/' }}), so they never need to reach Microsoft for Office files. Activation with a MAK key still contacts Microsoft once per PC.
